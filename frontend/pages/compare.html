What the results show

Reading the benchmark

Most efficient overall

HERF Hybrid leads the pack

HERF Hybrid records the lowest average at 4.200 guesses and the best worst case at 5 guesses in the five-code benchmark. It combines information gain, average candidate reduction, and worst-case protection.

Fastest execution

Knuth is quickest

Knuth has the lowest runtime at 0.002354 seconds. Its average of 5.774 guesses is higher than the leading hybrid strategies, showing the trade-off between speed and guess efficiency.

Baseline gap

Random is the outlier

Random needs 635.376 guesses on average and reaches 1,293 in the worst case. This gives a clear baseline for measuring how much strategy improves the game.
