How the numbers are calculated

N candidate codes All secrets still possible
→
Feedback groups Group secrets by B/W result

Count each group si secrets in group i
Probability pi = si / N Chance of each feedback
Entropy H = -Σ pi log2(pi) Expected information gained
Expected left E = Σ si2 / N Average candidates remaining
Ruled out (1 - E / N) × 100% Average candidates eliminated
Worst case max(si) Largest remaining group
HERF score 0.5H′ + 0.3R′ + 0.2M′ H′, R′, M′ are normalized scores

Decision criteria

HERF weights normalized entropy at 50% (information gain), expected elimination at 30% (average search-space reduction), and minimax reduction at 20% (worst-case protection). These are transparent heuristic priorities, not proven optimal weights.
